2. What the App does

The App lets one Android phone (the host) offer part of its storage to other nearby phones (guests). Phones and PCs connect directly to each other, using Google Nearby or the same Wi-Fi or hotspot network. The host decides how much space to offer, who may connect, and what each guest may do (view, upload, overwrite, delete, create folders). There is no cloud service: files exist only on the phones involved.

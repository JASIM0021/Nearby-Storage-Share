Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026 · Applies to the Android app Nearby Storage Share (package com.nearbyshare)

1. The short version

  • No accounts, no servers, no ads, no analytics. We do not run any server that receives your files or personal data.
  • Files and settings stay on the phones and PCs that use the app. Devices talk to each other directly - over Bluetooth / Wi-Fi when they are near each other, or over the same Wi-Fi or hotspot network - never through a server of ours.
  • When you host, other people's files are stored on your phone, and you (the owner of that phone) can see and delete them. When you upload to someone else's phone, that person can see and delete your files.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.
  • You can erase everything by deleting the app's data or uninstalling it.

2. Who is responsible

Nearby Storage Share is provided by Sk Jasimudin (Todayintech Solution) ("we", "us"). Because the app has no accounts or servers, we do not receive or hold the files, names or settings described below. They are held on your own devices, and on the devices you connect to.

4. What the other person can see

  • If you are the host: you can see the name and random ID of each phone that connects, the names, sizes and dates of the files it stores, and its activity in the security log. In "Visible folder" mode its files are saved in a folder you chose with their real names, so you and any app with access to that folder can read, change or delete them.
  • If you are a guest: the host can see your device name, your random ID, and the names, sizes and contents of the files you upload. Only upload what you are comfortable with that person having. The host can delete your files at any time.
  • Guests are kept apart: one guest cannot see another guest's files through the app.

6. How data is protected

  • Devices connect either through Google's Nearby Connections (encrypted) or over the local network using TLS (versions 1.2/1.3) with a certificate on each device. When two devices pair for the first time, both show the same 6-digit code that you compare before approving; after that each device recognises the other by its pinned certificate. The code is derived so that an attacker in the middle cannot make the two screens agree.
  • After pairing, each device proves its identity with a shared secret on every reconnect. The secret is stored encrypted with the Android Keystore.
  • The host checks every request against what that guest is allowed to do, so a modified app cannot do more than the host permits. Files are checked with a SHA-256 checksum, and incomplete uploads never appear as finished files.
  • Backups are switched off for the app's data so files and keys are not copied to cloud backups or transferred to a new phone automatically.
  • The app does not add its own encryption to files at rest. In private storage they are protected by Android's app sandbox and your phone's own storage encryption. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

The app uses Google Play services (Nearby Connections) to find and connect to nearby devices. This is a Google component on your phone and is governed by Google's Privacy Policy; Google may process connection-related information as described there. We do not control it. The app contains no advertising or analytics libraries and no other third-party data collection. See the open-source notices for the libraries used.

8. What we do not do

  • We do not collect, receive or store your files, device names or usage data on any server.
  • We do not sell, rent or trade personal information, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites.

9. Keeping and deleting data

  • Data stays on a phone until you delete it. In the app you can delete files (where permitted), revoke or block a device, clear finished transfers, or stop sharing at any time.
  • On a PC, delete the folder %APPDATA%\NearbyStorageShare (and uninstall the app) to remove everything the Windows app holds.
  • To remove everything the app holds on a phone, use Android Settings → Apps → Nearby Storage Share → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app.
  • Files you uploaded to another person's phone are on their device; ask them to delete them, or delete them yourself if the host allowed deleting.
  • If you chose a visible folder, files in it are ordinary files and stay there until deleted, even if you uninstall the app.

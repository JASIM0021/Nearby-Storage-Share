Nearby Storage Share

Turn spare phone storage into a private drive for people nearby. No cloud, no accounts.

  • Host a phone: choose how much space to share and who may connect.
  • Connect from another phone: browse, upload, open and save files, even from the Files app.
  • Stay in control: per-device permissions, storage limits and a security log, all enforced on the host.
  • Private by design: files travel directly between the two phones. We run no server.
